# TalentLens AI

AI-powered student profile and self-introduction platform (React + SweetAlert2).

## Run
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome or Edge.
   Optional: serve it locally with `npx serve .` or `python -m http.server`.

An internet connection is needed on first load (React, SweetAlert2, QR code and fonts load from CDNs).

## Features
- Sign up and log in (saved in your browser's localStorage)
- Profile builder: resume, photo, semester GPAs, projects, skills, certificates
- Automatic intro video made from your profile (browser text-to-speech or your own recorded voice)
- Resume strength score, job role matches and skill gaps
- Share link and QR code, plus a recruiter view with a snapshot

No backend: all data stays on your device.
